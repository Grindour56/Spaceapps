Module foundation · not an operational forecast

Observed fire activity is not fire hazard

Active-fire detections identify satellite-observed thermal anomalies. Hazard estimation needs weather, fuels, terrain and uncertainty-aware validation. No risk score is generated here.

Future input families

  • Temperature, relative humidity, wind and precipitation (e.g. ERA5-Land / national weather services)
  • Drought indices (e.g. SPEI, soil moisture)
  • Vegetation condition, land cover and fuel load (MODIS / Sentinel products)
  • Topography and historical fire occurrence

Required validation

  • Define target event and forecast lead time
  • Spatial and temporal hold-out testing
  • Calibration, uncertainty and false-alarm assessment
  • Independent operational review before early-warning claims
Not implemented: weather ingestion, fuel modeling, fire spread, or operational hazard prediction.
Future module design

Before-and-after event explorer

Hotspot coordinates are not fire perimeters. Burned-area estimates require independent imagery/products and explicit uncertainty.

  1. Select event AOI and time from the active-fire map/calendar.
  2. Acquire cloud-screened Sentinel-2 or Landsat imagery near user-chosen pre/post dates (no automatic download in this MVP).
  3. Compute documented NDVI/NBR and dNBR from surface reflectance; mask clouds and shadows.
  4. Compare with MODIS burned-area products where appropriate and report spatial/temporal uncertainty.
Not implemented: imagery search, raster processing, burned-area delineation, or vegetation-change metrics.
